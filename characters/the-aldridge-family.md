# THE ALDRIDGE FAMILY

## Overview

The family that lived at 1842 Harrow Creek Road before the evacuation.
They are never seen. They exist only through what they left behind.

## Members

| Name | Age (1980) | Details |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Daniel Aldridge | ~40s | Father. Built the bomb shelter. Obsessive. Loved his family. |
| Ruth Aldridge | ~40s | Mother. Careful handwriting. Labeled boxes. Kept the house. |
| Tommy Aldridge | 12 | Older boy. Model rockets. Guitar. Comics. Growth chart stops 1980. |
| Sammy Aldridge | 7 | Younger boy. Robot comforter. Toy soldiers. Bicycle with streamers. |

## What We Know About Them

- Evacuated August 11, 1980 with the rest of the town.
- Daniel had found the pre-existing Civil Defense shelter under the shed.
- He spent months stocking it: 6 cots, 90 days of MREs, water, filters.
- He planned to hide his family there and refuse the evacuation.
- He never did. They left with everyone else.
- The shelter was never used.

## Daniel's Journal (Key Entries)

- **Oct 14, 1978:** "The air smells like copper again. Tommy's cough is worse."
- **Mar 2, 1979:** "Found the shelter under the shed. Civil Defense. Six berths."
- **Aug 10, 1980:** "They're going door to door. Evacuation at six tomorrow.
  We go under when the trucks come. I'll turn the wheel from the inside."

## The "Copper Air"

Daniel's journal repeatedly mentions the air smelling/tasting like copper.
This is NOT chemical contamination. This is Cold Electricity fallout from the
Harrow Creek processing plant (an Abscissa Industries entropic exhaust vent).
Carol doesn't know this. The audience (if they've played Digital Decay) will.

## What They Left Behind

- The bicycle (Sammy's). Lying on its side. Streamers still knotted.
- The growth charts (both boys). Stopping in 1980.
- The coats on the wall rack. Nobody went back for them.
- The shelter. Fully stocked. Never used. Waiting.
