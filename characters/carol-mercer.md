# CAROL MERCER

## Overview

- **Age:** Mid-thirties
- **Role:** Urbex blogger / photographer / documentarian
- **Blog:** (Name TBD — see notes below)
- **Motivation:** Build the only complete record of a town the world forgot.
- **Personality:** Curious, respectful, a little funny, quietly unsettled.
  Never sensational. Talks to her audience like a friend is standing next to her.

## Appearance

- Heavy waxed-canvas hooded cloak. Waterproof. Worn soft at the edges.
- Practical boots. Mud-caked by Episode 2.
- DSLR... no. **Film camera** around her neck. Strap worn soft.
- No flashlight. LED lantern only.

| Item | Model / Type | Notes |

## The RV

- 1980s Winnebago. Battered, rusted, faded chevrons. One mirror held by a bolt.
- Stocked: canned food, water jugs, batteries, first-aid kit.
- Darkroom: blackout curtains, red safelight, chemical trays, drying line.
- Scanner: flatbed Epson on fold-down table. Light table. Loupe.
- Satellite dish: roof-mounted, green link light. Uploads blog entries.

## Voice Notes

- Contractions always. "It's," "don't," "that's."
- Self-corrections: "That's... that's actually kind of creepy."
- Dry humor to defuse tension: "Population... currently zero."
- Goes quiet when something hits. Doesn't narrate over emotion.
- Never says "haunted" unironically. Says "waiting."

## Arc Across the Season

- **Ep 1–3:** Excited. Professional. This is a job she loves.
- **Ep 4–7:** Curious. Starting to notice the pattern. The stasis field confuses her.
- **Ep 8–11:** Quieter. The town is getting to her. She's less performative.
- **Ep 12–13:** Reverent. She understands she's the only witness. The weight of it.
