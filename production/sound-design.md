# SOUND DESIGN — BLACKWOOD VALLEY

## Core Principle
Each location has its own acoustic signature. The audience should be able to
close their eyes and know where Carol is by the sound alone.

## The RV (Safe Space)
- Heater hum (constant, low, warm).
- Soft radio music (acoustic guitar, copyright-free).
- Rain on the roof (muffled, distant, comforting).
- Chemical slosh (darkroom). Drip of negatives.
- Scanner whir. Keyboard clicks.
- Satellite upload: a soft, digital chirp when the upload completes.

## The House — Ground Floor
- TV static hiss (constant, from the living room).
- Refrigerator compressor hum (deep, throaty, cycles on and off).
- Grandfather clock ticking (steady, patient, from the dining area).
- Carol's boots on carpet: muffled, soft, sinking.
- The house settling: occasional groans, creaks, pops.

## The House — Upstairs
- Fluorescent buzz (the one bathroom light). Angry. Electrical.
- Carpet muffles footsteps (worse than creaking — she has to listen to feel).
- Wood flexing under her weight. Slow. Deliberate.
- The attic draft: a low, hollow whistle.

## The Basement
- Hollow drips. Plink... plink... into a puddle.
- Low settling rumbles. The house above pressing down.
- Fridge hum transmitted from above (faint, distant, through the floor).
- Concrete echoes. Every sound bounces.
- Carol's boots on concrete: sharp, echoey.

## The Outdoors
- Rain. Always rain. Heavy, chaotic, loud.
- Wind through overgrown grass.
- The swing set chain creaking (one chain snapped, the seat swings).
- Mud sucking at boots.
- The shed door: SCREEECH.

## The Shed
- Rain on the roof (louder than inside the house, thinner walls).
- Cobwebs: a faint, papery rustle when the lantern beam passes.
- The trapdoor hinges: a high, metallic SCREAM.
- The rush of dry air from below.

## The Shelter (DEAD SILENCE)
- NO room tone. NO hum. NO tick. NO drip.
- Just Carol's breathing and the faint ozone smell (which you can't hear,
  but the audience should feel the absence of sound).
- The vault door closing: a deep, resonant THUD felt in the chest.
- The pressure change should be audible — ears popping, a slight whoosh.

## The Darkroom (End of Every Episode)
- Chemical slosh. Drip. The red safelight buzzing faintly.
- Scanner whir. Keyboard clicks.
- The satellite upload chirp.
- Then: silence. The heater. The rain. Sleep.

## Rules
- No music during exploration. Only diegetic sound.
- No musical stings for scares. No jump-scare audio.
- The tape recorder hiss is always present when Carol is recording.
- The film camera CLACK-CHHK is loud, mechanical, physical.
- The strobe flash leaves a faint ringing in the ears.
