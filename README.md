# Blackwood Valley

A 13-episode analog-horror / urban-exploration web series.

Carol Mercer, an urbex blogger, moves into the evacuated town of Blackwood Valley,
Washington, living out of her RV for weeks. She explores the sealed town one
location at a time, documenting what the rest of the world forgot — and slowly
realizing that some things in this town refuse to decay.

## Structure

- `story.md` — the series bible (premise, themes, structure)
- `episodes/` — 13 episode scripts, scene cards, production notes
- `characters/` — character sheets
- `worldbuilding/` — locations, the stasis field, the RV
- `plot/` — season arc, central mystery, timeline
- `continuity/` — easter eggs, unresolved mysteries, photo log
- `lore/` — Abscissa Industries connections, the plant, the evacuation
- `production/` — sound design, camera notes, darkroom ritual, music
- `glossary/`, `style-sheet.md`

## The Naming Convention

Each episode is titled: **"The [Place] That [Poignant Action]"**

1. The House That Never Turned Off
2. The School That Stopped Mid-Lesson
3. The Station That Held Its Breath
4. The Church That Waited for Sunday
5. The Precinct That Filed Its Last Report
6. The Library That Kept Its Books
7. The Town Hall That Signed the Order
8. The Firehouse That Answered Its Last Call
9. The Diner That Poured Its Last Cup
10. The Clinic That Kept Its Hours
11. The Post Office That Held Its Letters
12. The Plaza That Waited for Everyone
13. The Plant That Changed the Air

## Shared Universe

Blackwood Valley exists in the same universe as:
- **Digital Decay** (survival horror game — Pine Valley, Ohio)
- **The Abandoned Horizon** (digital-decay / liminal-space survival horror novel)

Connections are embedded as easter eggs. See `lore/abscissa-connection.md`.
