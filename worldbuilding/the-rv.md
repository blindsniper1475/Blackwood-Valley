# THE RV — CAROL'S MOBILE HQ

## Overview
- 1980s Winnebago. Battered, rusted, faded sun-bleached chevrons.
- One side mirror held on with a bolt. Ugly, dented, running perfectly.
- This is Carol's home for the entire season. She does not leave the town.

## Exterior
- Compact satellite dish on the roof. Deploys mechanically. Green link light.
- Faded paint. Rust at wheel arches. Mud-caked tires.
- A small American flag sticker, peeling, on the rear bumper.

## Interior — Living Area
- Fold-down table. Bunk. Small galley counter.
- Heater (hums constantly). Radio (acoustic guitar tracks, copyright-free).
- Shelves: canned food, water jugs, batteries, first-aid kit.
- Map of Blackwood County taped above the galley. Red marker circles.
  One circle in Ohio: "Pine Valley, OH — check E. Cross blog first."

## Interior — Darkroom Section (Rear)
- Blackout curtains sealed with duct tape.
- Red safelight bulb (clipped to ceiling).
- Chemical trays: developer, stop bath, fixer.
- Clothesline with binder clips (drying negatives).
- Towel on the floor (catches drips).
- Smells like vinegar and fixer.

## Interior — Scanning Station
- Flatbed scanner (chunky 2000s Epson). Plugged into power inverter.
- Light table (thin glowing white panel).
- Loupe (for inspecting negatives).
- Laptop (2013 era, sturdy). Blog editor. Satellite uplink software.

## Power
- Runs off the RV battery + a small generator.
- Satellite dish draws minimal power. Green light blinks when connected.
- Enough for the scanner, laptop, safelight, and heater. Not much else.

## Sound Signature
- Heater hum. Soft radio music. Rain on the roof.
- The slosh of chemicals in the developing tank.
- The drip of negatives on the clothesline.
- The mechanical whir of the flatbed scanner.
- The quiet click of the keyboard.
- Outside: rain. Always rain.
