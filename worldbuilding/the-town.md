# BLACKWOOD VALLEY — TOWN PROFILE

## Population and Scale

- **Pre-evacuation population:** approximately 3,500 residents.
- **Households:** approximately 1,200, per the county evacuation estimate.
- **Evacuation:** mandatory order effective 6:00 AM on August 11, 1980.
- **Carol's arrival:** October 12, 2013. The town has no resident population.
- Treat the population and household figures as rounded planning estimates, not
  an exact census count. They describe the town before evacuation.

Blackwood is a small, self-contained town with enough residents to support its
own school, clinic, police precinct, firehouse, library, town hall, church,
shops, and homes. The thirteen episodes visit selected anchor sites; Carol does
not document every household.

## Carol's Stay

Carol lives in her Winnebago inside the evacuated town for about eight weeks,
roughly two months. The season begins on October 12, 2013. The first two
episodes happen on consecutive days; later episodes can skip days while she
waits out weather, develops film, researches records, and prepares the next
site. The exact date of the finale remains open until the episode timeline is
locked.

## Public Record Trail

- The county's 1980 evacuation material gives the rounded estimate of 3,500
  residents and 1,200 households.
- A 2010 public notice says air monitoring cleared the contamination concern.
- The evacuation order itself was never formally rescinded. Carol can report
  what the public documents say, but she does not learn the hidden cause.
