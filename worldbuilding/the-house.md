# 1842 HARROW CREEK ROAD — THE ALDRIDGE HOUSE

## Overview
- Two-story Victorian-style house. Decrepit. Not haunted. Waiting.
- Built: ~1920s. Occupied by the Aldridge family from ~1974 to August 1980.
- Power: Still live. Never shut off.
- Condition: Organic materials decay. Infrastructure preserved (stasis field).

## Exterior
- Covered porch, sagging. Two columns, peeling white paint.
- Rusted wrought-iron grille between columns. Rust bleeding onto boards.
- Rusted metal porch chair (canvas seat rotted).

- HOME SWEET HOME mat (rotted). Screen door (torn mesh). Brass "1842."
- Porch light: dark, cracked glass. Dead.
