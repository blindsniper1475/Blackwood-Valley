# THE STASIS FIELD

## What It Is

A spotty, localized preservation effect caused by ambient Cold Electricity
fallout from the Harrow Creek processing plant. It follows buried infrastructure
(wiring, plumbing, underground conduits) and does NOT affect organic or
loose materials.

Carol doesn't know what it is. She just knows some things in the town
refuse to decay, and other things rot normally. She notices the pattern
but can't explain it.

## Rules

1. **Underground = Preserved.** The bomb shelter, buried pipes, the foundation.
2. **Wired into the walls = Preserved.** The electrical grid, outlets, the
   bathroom light circuit, the grandfather clock's power cord.
3. **Organic / loose = Decays.** Furniture, clothes, paper, food, carpet.
4. **Outdoors = Decays.** Grass, wood, metal exposed to rain.
5. **The pattern is inconsistent.** Carol can't fully map it. Some rooms
   in the same house are preserved while adjacent rooms rot.

## What's Preserved (By Episode)

| Location | Preserved | Decaying |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| The House | Electrical grid, fridge, TV, clock, bathroom light | Couch, carpet, clothes, photos, food |
| The Shelter | Everything (MREs, cots, blankets, calendar, logbook) | N/A — fully sealed |
| The Attic | Nothing | Boxes, dress, albums, furniture |
| The Woodshop | Nothing | Sawdust, tools, birdhouses, lumber |
| The Outdoors | Nothing | Grass, shed, swing set, bicycle |

## Carol's Observation (Ep 4 or 5)

"Okay, so here's what's weird. The stuff that's underground or wired into
the walls... it's fine. The clock, the fridge, the shelter. But anything
organic, anything loose, anything that was just sitting out... it's gone.
It's like the town is preserving its own skeleton but letting the flesh rot."

## The Audience Knows (But Carol Doesn't)

The stasis field is caused by Cold Electricity — the energy of the Hollow
Spaces. The Harrow Creek plant was an Abscissa Industries entropic exhaust
vent. The "contamination" that evacuated the town was Cold Electricity
fallout. The stasis isn't supernatural. It's physics. Just not our physics.
