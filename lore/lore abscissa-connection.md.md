# ABSCISSA INDUSTRIES — THE BLACKWOOD CONNECTION

## What Abscissa Industries Is (From Digital Decay Lore)

A shadowy corporate conglomerate / occult society founded in the 1920s.
They harness "Cold Electricity" from the Hollow Spaces (a non-Euclidean
entropic dimension) by building resonance facilities on ley line intersections.
They harvest human emotional energy to feed The Architect (a cosmic parasite).

## What Harrow Creek Is

The Harrow Creek Processing Plant is NOT a harvesting node.
It is NOT an experiment zone.
It is the **entropic exhaust vent** — the "grounding wire."

Abscissa's harvesting nodes (like Pine Valley's mall, school, hospital)
generate massive amounts of Cold Electricity and Rot. That energy has to
go somewhere. Harrow Creek was built to safely vent the excess.

The "air contamination" that evacuated Blackwood Valley in 1980 was
Cold Electricity fallout. Not chemical. Not nuclear. Dimensional.

## Why the Town Was Sealed

Standard Abscissa protocol: when a location reaches critical contamination,
they condemn/quarantine/seal it under the guise of an environmental hazard.
In Pine Valley, it was "structural failure" and "biohazard lockdown."
In Blackwood Valley, it was "air contamination from the processing plant."

The evacuation order was never rescinded because Abscissa didn't care
enough to file the paperwork. The town was sealed and forgotten.
Not by malice. By inertia.

## Why Carol Never Learns the Full Truth

Carol is a documentarian, not an occultist. She finds the word "Abscissa"
on a letterhead. She notices the stasis pattern. She reads Daniel's journal
about the "copper air." But she doesn't know about the Hollow Spaces,
The Architect, Cold Electricity, or ley lines.

She gets 80% of the truth. The audience gets 100%.
That gap is where the horror lives.

## The Spotty Stasis Field

The Cold Electricity fallout didn't saturate the town evenly.
It followed buried infrastructure — wiring, plumbing, underground conduits.
Where it pooled, things were preserved (stasis). Where it didn't reach,
things decayed normally.

This is why the shelter is pristine but the attic is rotting.
This is why the fridge hums but the couch molds.
The town's skeleton is preserved. Its flesh is not.
