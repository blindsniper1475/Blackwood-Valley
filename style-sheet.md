# BLACKWOOD VALLEY — STYLE SHEET

## Voice

- Carol speaks conversationally. Contractions. Self-corrections. Dry humor.
- She never monologues. She talks TO someone (her audience).
- She gets genuinely excited about mundane details (a growth chart, a clock).
- She gets quiet when something hits her emotionally. She doesn't narrate over it.
- She never says "spooky," "creepy," or "haunted" unironically.

## Visual Language

- Warm LED lantern glow vs. cold blue moonlight/rain.
- Camera flash is blinding, physical, momentary. It reveals then vanishes.
- The RV interior is warm, cramped, lived-in. Red safelight in the darkroom.
- Film grain. Shallow depth of field. 35mm lens feel.

## Sound Language

- Each location has its own acoustic signature.
- The RV is the "safe" sound: heater hum, soft radio, rain on the roof.
- The shelter is DEAD SILENCE. No room tone. Pressure change.
- Carol's footsteps change per surface: carpet (muffled), tile (sharp),
  concrete (echo), dirt (soft), wood (creak).

## Formatting Conventions

- Episode titles: "The [Place] That [Poignant Action]"
- Blog entries tagged: "Entry N"
- Dates written: October 12, 2013 / October 13, 2013
- Times written: 10:45 PM / 12:15 AM
- Sound cues in CAPS: CLICK-WHIRR, CLANG, TICK-TOCK
- Carol's dialogue in CAPS: **CAROL:**
- Voiceover marked: **CAROL (V.O.):**

## Things Carol Never Does

- She never touches personal items unless photographing them.
- She never opens the logbook in the shelter (she reads it, then puts it back).
- She never speculates about ghosts. She speculates about bureaucracy.
- She never leaves the town. The RV is home. The town is the project.

## Things the Script Never Does

- No jump scares.
- No supernatural reveals.
- No music during exploration (only diegetic sound).
- No resolution of the two unresolved mysteries (the fridge, the attic ladder).
- No explanation of the stasis field. Carol notices the pattern. She doesn't solve it.
