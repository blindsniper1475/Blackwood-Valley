# EASTER EGGS — SHARED UNIVERSE CONNECTIONS

These are subtle references to **Digital Decay** (Pine Valley, Ohio) and
**The Abandoned Horizon** embedded in Blackwood Valley. Carol never
acknowledges them directly. The audience discovers them.

## Episode 1 — The House

| Easter Egg | Location | Details |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| The Map | RV interior | One red circle is in Ohio. Handwritten note: "Pine Valley — check E. Cross's blog first." |
| The Copper Air | Daniel's journal | "The air smells like copper." = Cold Electricity hallmark from Digital Decay. |
| The Letterhead | Father's study | Among clippings: a faded corporate letterhead reading "Westfield Properties & Logistics" (an Abscissa front). |
| The Flash Fails | Bomb shelter | Carol's camera flash produces a sickly blue-purple light when photographing the deepest part of the shelter. Cold Electricity interference. |
| The Static | Living room TV | The TV plays static. Not just no signal — the static has a faint, rhythmic pulse. Like breathing. Like the Hollow Spaces. |

## Episode 13 — The Plant (Planned)

| Easter Egg | Location | Details |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| The Logo | Plant entrance | The Abscissa Industries logo, weathered but visible. Carol photographs it without knowing what it is. |

## Rules for Easter Eggs

- Carol NEVER recognizes them. She's not in on the joke.
- They must be visible in the frame but not highlighted.
- No dialogue references. Visual only.
- If a viewer hasn't played Digital Decay, the easter eggs look like set dressing.
- If they HAVE played it, they lose their minds.
