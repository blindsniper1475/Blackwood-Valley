# UNRESOLVED MYSTERIES

These are deliberately left unanswered. No reveal. No payoff.
Carol notices them. She moves on. So does the viewer.

## 1. The Refrigerator

The avocado-green fridge is still running. Still cold. The compressor
kicks on. It's been running for 33 years.

**Carol's reaction:** "That compressor should have seized decades ago."
She doesn't theorize. She moves on.

**The real answer (audience only):** The fridge is on a circuit that runs
along a buried conduit carrying Cold Electricity. The stasis field keeps
the compressor functioning. It's not supernatural. It's just not our physics.

## 2. The Attic Ladder

The pull-down ladder to the attic is already halfway down when Carol arrives.

**Carol's reaction:** "Somebody pulled it down and didn't finish."
She doesn't investigate further. She pulls it the rest of the way.

**The real answer:** Unknown. Deliberately unknown. Maybe Daniel was up
there before the evacuation. Maybe the house settled. Maybe it's nothing.
The audience is meant to sit with the discomfort of not knowing.

## 3. The Bathroom Light

One fluorescent tube in the upstairs bathroom is still on. Buzzing. Flickering.

**Carol's reaction:** "Bulb should have burned out. Ballast should have gone."
She takes a photo and leaves.

**The real answer:** The light is on a circuit that runs through the stasis
field. The bulb and ballast are preserved by Cold Electricity. But Carol
doesn't know that. She just knows it's wrong.

## Rules

- These are NEVER explained in dialogue.
- Carol notices, reacts, and moves on.
- No musical sting. No camera lingering. Just Carol's voice, then the next room.
- The audience is meant to carry these questions home.
